- [ ] **More variance in where hostiles and asteroids appear, from the front 270 degrees (idea 2026-11-21):** today (all 8-bit versions, `docs/_ship_spawning.md`) a hostile starts at x, y, z of about 12,800 in one of four diagonal corners ahead, an asteroid within about ±2,300 of the centre line at z 12,800, and everything only ahead (the sign of z is never set). Wanted: a random distance and direction for both. **Hostiles:** from the front 270 degrees (everything except the back 90 degrees cone), flying towards the player. **Asteroids:** any position around the player (no back cone) and any heading (decided 2026-11-21: they drift, they are not aimed). Design thoughts:
  - **Position without trigonometry:** random high bytes for x, y and z (for example x_hi, y_hi random 0 to `&3F`, z_hi random `&20`-`&3F`, so the distance varies between about 8,000 and 16,000) and random signs for all three. The back cone is spared with one comparison: if z is negative and both x_hi and y_hi are smaller than z_hi, flip the z sign (or draw again). That leaves the front 180 degrees plus the two 45 degree side wedges behind the lateral axis = 270 degrees (as a square pyramid, close enough to a cone). Asteroids skip the test: random signs for x, y and z are enough.
  - **Heading:** hostiles aim at the player through their own tactics. Asteroids have no AI (`spawnCommand0` clears it) and keep the identity orientation, i.e. they all fly along +z; for a random heading give each one a random orientation: `setDirectionofEntity` at `&82B8` builds the matrix from a direction vector, so a random vector (the signed random bytes, no normalising needed if the routine copes) may be enough. Fully random headings mean that many asteroids spawned at 12,800 units fly away and never come near; if that feels empty, bias the heading towards a point near the player instead (the ST points its asteroids at the player), but that is a choice for later.
  - **Packs:** `spawnEscortPack` calls `spawnShip` per ship, so with variance the wingmen would scatter over the sky: spawn the leader with the variance and put the others at the leader's position plus a small random offset.
  - **Where the code goes:** `spawnShip` is in the resident glue (3 bytes free before `&9C80`, 13 before `&9D9E`, 9 in the `bankInit` overlay): the new rules replace the fixed `&32` and the pirate-only random high bytes (about 14 bytes today), but the back-cone test and the point-at call may need a far-call module (bank 6 has about 2.3 KB of code space) or a gap found in the glue; measure first.
  - **Checks:** the sampling script of the pirates item (call `spawnShip` 80 times, histogram of the signs and of the distance), a test that no spawn lands in the back cone, screenshots in the front, side and rear views; the missions (Constrictor, Thargoid, the marked Asp, Trumbles) and the safe zone must behave as before; frame time with ships at the sides (the fill clamps to the viewport).
  - **Must be a compile-time switch** (`-DSPAWN_VARIANCE`; in the 128K build set or unset in `src/elite128/config.inc`, like the other flags): it is a deliberate difference from the original game, so with the flag off the spawn code and its bytes stay exactly as they are, and `src/elite` stays byte-exact (`check_src.sh`). Decide the default for the 128K disk and whether `main` gets the switch too.
