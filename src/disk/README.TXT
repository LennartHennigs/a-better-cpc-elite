ELITE - MORE MISSIONS EDITION
-----------------------------
Amstrad CPC 464 / 6128

To play:  RUN"ELITE

The original game had 3 missions.
This edition has 8. The five new
ones come from the BBC disc, C64
and NES versions and from the PC
game Elite Plus.

Find them yourself: fly, fight,
trade, dock and listen to what
the stations tell you.

Also new:
ESC leaves the buy, sell, and
equipment menus.
A sells your whole hold.
Cash shows while you buy.
Kills show on the status page.
Planets and suns hide stars.
Pirates come from more sides.
New ships fly the lanes, and
space is busier.

You can navigate the game's
ships in the start menu via
arrow keys.

Tools on this disk:
RUN"SHOWSHPS  every ship in 3D
RUN"TRAINER   cheats
RUN"EDITSAVE  edit a saved CMDR
RUN"LISTSAVE  list saved CMDRs
RUN"CONVSAVE  convert CMDR saves

Elite by David Braben and Ian
Bell. CPC version by Firebird.

Good luck, Commander.
